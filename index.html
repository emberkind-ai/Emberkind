Your data

Everything you create here is saved only on this device. It isn't sent to us and we can't see it, so it isn't backed up either: clearing the app or browser data, or switching device, starts fresh. To keep a copy, tap Back up my data. It saves a file to your device, so keep it somewhere safe, like your Files app, or email it to yourself. Restore a backup loads it back, even on a new phone. Adding Emberkind to your home screen helps keep it safe. No ads, no selling, no tracking.
