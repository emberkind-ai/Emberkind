Delete your Emberkind data

Emberkind is an AI wellbeing companion app by Aevum Awareness Academy. You can delete your data at any time and keep your account.

How to delete your data

  1. Open Emberkind and go to You.
  2. Choose Your data, then Delete my data, and confirm.

If you're signed in, this deletes your data from your phone and from your account at the same time, and you stay signed in. Without an account, your data only exists on your phone, so deleting it there removes it completely.

What gets deleted

  • Your check-ins, journal entries, practice history and routines
  • Your Embervale and settings
  • Your conversations with companions

What is kept

Your account (name, email address, Google account identifier and profile photo), so you can keep signing in. To remove those too, delete your account.

How long it takes

Deletion happens straight away. Our hosting provider's automatic recovery backups may keep a copy for up to 30 days, after which it expires. Reports you sent about a reply are kept for up to 90 days, then deleted automatically.

By email

If you can't use the app, email emberkindai@gmail.com from the email address on your account and we'll delete your data within 7 days.
