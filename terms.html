Health and AI

Emberkind is designed for wellbeing, self-understanding and nervous system regulation: a place to settle, notice patterns, and find ideas for routines, conversations and nourishment, especially in the moments when people aren't available.

It is not medical care, therapy, counselling, psychological treatment, bodywork, dietetics or a crisis service. It doesn't diagnose, treat or cure any condition, and it isn't a substitute for your GP, psychologist, psychiatrist, counsellor, dietitian, bodyworker or any other clinical or non-clinical professional.

It also isn't just entertainment. We take what you bring seriously, and we built it to be genuinely useful. But your health decisions are yours to make with people qualified to help you.

If you're living with a mental or physical health condition, keep working with your care team, and talk with them about how Emberkind fits alongside their support.

Soma, Siro, Ava and Orra are AI companions, not people. Their replies are generated by an AI model, which means they can:

  • misunderstand what you mean
  • be inaccurate, incomplete or out of date
  • sound more certain than they should

Treat every conversation as a supportive chat, not as facts, evidence, a diagnosis, treatment advice or a care plan. Use what's helpful for reflection, regulation and ideas, and check with a qualified professional before acting on any plan they suggest.

Companions don't remember you between devices, and they can't contact anyone on your behalf. If something they say feels wrong or upsetting, stop the conversation; you can always reach a real person through Need a person.

Breathwork safety

Breathe gently and within your comfort. You never need to force a breath.

  • Stop and breathe normally if you feel dizzy, lightheaded, tingly, breathless or more anxious.
  • If you're pregnant, or have a heart, lung, blood pressure, seizure or panic condition, check with your doctor before breathwork.
  • Never practise while driving, operating machinery, or in or near water.
  • If closing your eyes doesn't feel safe, keep them open. Every practice works either way.

Food and nourishment

Ava shares general ideas about food, energy and nourishment, drawing on everyday nutrition, traditional practices and gut-brain science. These are ideas to explore, not dietary, medical or nutritional advice.

Before changing how you eat, talk with your GP or an Accredited Practising Dietitian, especially if you have allergies or intolerances, a medical condition, take medication, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have a history of disordered eating. For eating disorder support, the Butterfly Foundation is on 1800 33 4673.
