EMBERKIND: FUTURE PRODUCT DIRECTION
(A direction to explore later. Nothing here is built, and none of it is part of the current release.)

The vision
  A fully realised 3D Emberkind Village: one continuous world where each tab of the app leads to a place in it.
  Home is the fire where the companions sit; Regulate leads to the elemental places (the hearth, the river, the
  open sky, the earth and the night sky above the valley); Embervale is the village itself, growing with you;
  Tools and You live in buildings of their own. Moving between tabs means moving through the world.

Why it isn't built now
  The current app draws its world in 2D (Canvas and DOM), with one small WebGL layer for the night sky and the
  wormhole into meditation. Convincing camera travel through a believable world needs real 3D assets, lighting
  and a camera that moves through geometry. Faking that in 2D read as zooms and fades rather than travel, so in
  2.7.3 those transitions were removed in favour of stability and simplicity. Only the rise into the night sky
  when a meditation begins remains.

What it would likely take
  - A dedicated 3D rendering architecture, for example React Native with a suitable 3D engine (or a web engine
    such as three.js or Babylon.js, or a game engine), chosen after prototyping and benchmarking on mid-range phones.
  - Real 3D art: terrain, the village buildings at each level, the companions, water, sky and weather, day and night.
  - A world/scene manager that keeps one camera, one clock (time of day and season) and one audio mix across tabs.
  - Performance budgets per device class, with a simple 2D fallback that keeps every practice usable.
  - A staged path: prototype one place (the hearth) end to end, measure it on real phones, then extend.

Ground rules when it happens
  Practices, timers, audio and the companions must keep working exactly as now at every stage. The world serves
  the practices, never the other way round.
