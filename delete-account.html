Delete your Emberkind account

You can delete your account, and everything stored with it, at any time. There are three ways.

1. In the app

Open Emberkind, go to You, then Account, and choose Delete my account.

2. On this page

Loading sign-in…

3. By email

Email emberkindai@gmail.com from the email address on your account. We'll delete it within 7 days and let you know.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, email address, Google account identifier and profile photo
  • Everything saved to your account (check-ins, journal, practice, Embervale, settings and conversations)
  • Your sign-in sessions

How long it takes

Deletion happens straight away. Our hosting provider's automatic recovery backups may keep a copy for up to 30 days, after which it expires. Reports you sent about a reply are kept for up to 90 days, then deleted automatically.

To delete your data but keep your account, see Delete your Emberkind data.
