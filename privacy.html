Emberkind Privacy Policy

Privacy policy. Last updated 7 November 2026.

Emberkind is an independent Australian app based in Sydney, New South Wales. In this policy, "Emberkind", "we" and "us" mean the Emberkind team. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles, and with NSW health privacy law where it applies.

How your data is stored

Unless you sign in, everything you create (your name, conversations, Embers, practice history, Embervale progress, check-ins and settings) is saved only on this device, in the app's own storage. It isn't sent to us, and we can't see it. If you sign in, it's also saved to your account (see "Your account"). That also means it isn't backed up: clearing the app or browser data, using private browsing, or switching device or browser starts fresh.

When you talk with a companion, the messages you send, along with some of your recent Emberkind activity (see "AI companions and overseas processing" below), are sent through Emberkind's secure server to our AI provider, currently Claude by Anthropic, to create a reply. If you report a reply, that report is sent too (see "Reports you send"). If you choose to share a photo or file with a companion, it is sent too (see "Photos and files you share"). To help you find conversations again, we also ask our AI provider to write a short title for each one, using its first few messages. If you tap Save as Ember, the recent conversation is sent so it can suggest a line to keep. If you ask Task buddy to break a task down, the task you typed and your current steps are sent so it can suggest small steps. Nothing else leaves your device.

What we collect, and why

Only what you choose to enter, used only to run Emberkind for you: the name you choose, your conversations, Embers you keep, your practices and check-ins, Embervale progress, settings, and any Community posts you choose to share.

Sensitive information

Conversations can include health and mental health details, which the law treats as sensitive information. By choosing to share them, you consent to us handling them only to provide your conversations. We never use them for advertising, never sell them, and never share them except as described here or where the law requires.

Local weather (optional)

If you turn on Match my local weather in Settings, Emberkind asks your device for its approximate location. On your device we round it to about 10 km and send only those rounded coordinates directly to Open-Meteo, a weather service, to get the current conditions (sunny, cloudy, rain or snow). Your location is never sent to us, is not stored by Emberkind, is not linked to your account, and is not used for anything else. We keep only the weather result, on your device, for up to 30 minutes. Open-Meteo receives the rounded coordinates and the usual technical details of a web request, and its own privacy policy applies to that request. This feature is entirely optional. You can turn it off at any time in Settings, and you can withdraw location permission in your device or browser settings.

What we never do

No ads. No selling or renting your data. No advertising trackers or analytics. No sharing with data brokers.

AI companions and overseas processing

Your companions are AI, not people. When a companion replies, your side of the conversation is processed by an AI provider to write the reply. Our provider is currently Anthropic (Claude). If we ever change or add an AI provider, we'll update this policy first and ask for your agreement before any of your messages go to them.

What is sent: your messages, the recent conversation with that companion, the companion's instructions and the name you chose. To help the companion respond well, some of your recent Emberkind activity is also included automatically: check-ins from the past week (your weather, energy, tags and the start of any note), your recent practice and your badge count. Anything you choose to talk through, such as a journal entry or routine, is included too. Two smaller requests send only what they need: Save as Ember sends the recent conversation, and Task buddy's Help me break it down sends the task and your current steps.

How: messages go through Emberkind's secure server, which holds our access key and passes them on. The server doesn't store the content of your messages, and your conversation history stays on your device.

Overseas: Anthropic processes this data mainly in the United States, so it is disclosed to an overseas recipient. We use Anthropic's business terms, which restrict how it can use your data, and we only send what is needed to create a reply.

Retention and training: under Anthropic's current business terms, messages sent this way aren't used to train its AI models. It may keep them for a limited period (currently up to about 30 days) to provide the service and detect misuse, and for longer if a message is flagged as a policy violation or the law requires it.

Safety: messages are scanned automatically for words that suggest risk, so the companion can respond with care. No person at Emberkind reads your conversations, and we don't contact anyone on your behalf. If a message suggests you may be at risk, the companion is instructed to encourage immediate support, and Emberkind shows Australian crisis services. Replies can be wrong and aren't medical, psychological, legal or financial advice.

Your choice: we ask for your agreement before conversations begin, and you can stop at any time. Companion chats are optional; the rest of Emberkind works without them.

Photos and files you share

In a companion chat you can choose to share up to three photos or PDF files with a message. Sharing is always your choice; nothing is taken from your device without you selecting it.

Where they go: the photo or file is sent with that one message, securely through Emberkind's server, to our AI provider (currently Anthropic's Claude) so your companion can see it and reply. Photos are made smaller on your device first. Anthropic handles them under the same terms described above, including overseas processing in the United States and not using them to train its models.

What's kept: Emberkind's server does not store your photos or files. A small preview (or, for a PDF, its name) is saved on your device with that chat so you can see what you shared. Deleting the chat, starting fresh, or deleting your data removes it.

Please take care: only share photos of other people with their permission, and avoid sharing documents with ID numbers, bank details or medical records. Companions can't provide professional advice about what's in a photo or document.

Hosting and technical information

Emberkind is hosted by Cloudflare. To deliver the app and protect it from misuse, Cloudflare processes basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type, and may keep it briefly in its logs. This may happen outside Australia. We don't use it to track you or for advertising.

Our server keeps a daily count of messages linked to your network (IP) address and an anonymous app ID for up to two days, only to apply fair-use limits and prevent misuse. Our server doesn't store the content of your messages.

Your account

You can use Emberkind with or without an account. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address, profile picture and a Google account identifier with us. While you're signed in, everything you create in Emberkind (your check-ins, journal, practice history, Embervale, settings, profile photo and your conversations with companions) is saved to your account on Emberkind's server, hosted by Cloudflare, so it's safe if you clear your browser or change phones. Without an account, it all stays only on your device. You can delete your account at any time in You, or at emberkind.emberkindai.workers.dev/delete-account.html, which removes everything saved with it. Signing in uses Google's sign-in service, under Google's own privacy policy. When you delete your data or your account, it's removed from our live systems straight away. Our hosting provider's automatic recovery backups may keep a copy for up to 30 days, after which it expires.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, your phone creates a private notification address for Emberkind through its push service (Google for Android, Apple for iPhone). We keep that address, the reminder times you choose and your time zone on our server, hosted by Cloudflare, so reminders arrive on time. Reminder messages are general prompts and never include your check-ins, journal or conversations. Turning reminders off in You, blocking notifications in your phone settings, or deleting your data removes the address from our server.

Reports you send

If a companion's reply seems harmful, wrong, upsetting, not helpful enough or out of character, you can report it from the reply's menu. A report sends us the reply you reported, the reason you chose, any note you add, which companion it was, the app release and the time. If you tick the box, it also includes your own message just before that reply, for context. Reports don't include your name or contact details, so please don't add personal details to your note.

Reports are stored securely with Cloudflare for up to 90 days, read only by the Emberkind team, and used only to improve how the companions respond and to keep them safe. We may receive an email alert about a new report, which includes the companion, the reason and a short excerpt of the reply, but never your note or your own message. Email alerts are sent through Resend (United States) to our Google email account.

Your choices

You can view, export and delete your data at any time from You, then Your data. You can stop using Emberkind whenever you like.

Age

Emberkind is designed for adults aged 18 and over.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. Data saved on your device is only as secure as the device itself, so use a screen lock.
